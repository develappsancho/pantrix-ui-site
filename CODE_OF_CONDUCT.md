# Code of Conduct

Türkçesi: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.tr.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.tr.md)

## Our pledge

Pantrix UI and its site are made by people who come to them from different places and with different experience.
Everyone taking part is treated with respect, and treats others the same way.

## What is expected

- Being kind and patient, above all with someone new.
- Disagreeing about the work, not about the person, and giving reasons.
- Taking a review's points calmly, and owning one's mistakes.
- Staying on the topic, and assuming good intent.
- Respecting people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Insults, mockery, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual, violent or demeaning language or images.
- Discrimination against anyone for who they are. This includes their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex, gender
  identity or expression, sexual orientation, nationality, religion, experience or education.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Going on with any of these after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in all of the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions. It also applies
wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report what you saw or went through directly to the maintainer,
[@developersancho](https://github.com/developersancho). A report is kept confidential, and someone who reports in good
faith is protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and answers it. Depending on what happened, the answer can be:

1. **A private word,** saying what was wrong.
2. **A warning,** with what happens if it goes on.
3. **A ban for a set time** from the project's spaces.
4. **A permanent ban.**

Content that breaks this code can be removed or edited.
